簡介About

楊朝棟教授現任東海大學教務長暨資訊工程學系終身特聘教授，並擔任中華民國大專校院資訊服務協會（ISAC）常務理事。曾任東海大學資訊工程學系系主任、圖書暨資訊處圖資長、圖書館館長、電子計算機中心主任，以及國家高速網路與計算中心兼任研究員。

研究專長為雲端計算、大數據、智慧物聯網與平行暨分散式處理。近年研究聚焦於雲端與大數據的系統架構與服務、邊緣運算上的深度學習部署、智慧醫療影像與臨床決策輔助，以及空氣品質、水質與電能的監測和預測。已發表期刊、專書章節與會議論文 350 篇以上，並主編兩本專書。

曾獲中華民國資訊學會「資訊榮譽獎章」與「李國鼎穿石獎」、中國工程師學會「傑出工程教授獎」、中國電機工程學會「傑出電機工程教授獎」、中華工程教育學會「教學傑出獎」及資訊月「傑出資訊人才獎」，並連續十七年（2010–2026）獲國科會補助大專校院獎勵特殊優秀人才。

English biography

Dr. Chao-Tung Yang is a Lifetime Distinguished Professor of Computer Science and the Dean of Academic Affairs at Tunghai University, Taichung, Taiwan. He received his B.Sc. in Computer Science from Tunghai University in 1990, and his M.Sc. (1992) and Ph.D. (1996) in Computer Science from National Chiao Tung University, where his dissertation won the 1996 Acer Dragon Award. From 1996 to 2001 he was an Associate Researcher in the Ground System Section of the National Space Organization. He joined Tunghai University in 2001, becoming Professor in 2007, Distinguished Professor in 2015 and Lifetime Distinguished Professor in 2019.

His research covers cloud computing, big data, parallel and multicore computing, AIoT and applied machine learning. He has published more than 350 papers and edited two books, Innovative Computing (LNEE 675, 2020) and Advances on P2P, Parallel, Grid, Cloud and Internet Computing (LNDECT 24, 2019). He serves on the editorial boards of Future Generation Computer Systems, International Journal of Communication Systems and KSII Transactions on Internet and Information Systems, and is a senior member of the IEEE.
